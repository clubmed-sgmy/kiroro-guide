Welcome!

Since 1950, Club Med has reinvented the way people travel, with one simple goal in mind: to bring happiness, peace of mind and complete freedom to all our guests.

In a world that never seems to slow down, we believe free time is the most precious thing we can offer. With nearly 70 Premium All Inclusive beach and mountain resorts around the world, we take care of every detail so you can practise The Art of Free Time: simple pleasures, unhurried tables and moments worth savouring.

Our friendly international G.O team is here to help you make the most of every moment. So go on, try something new. Welcome to Club Med Kiroro Peak, part of our Exclusive Collection, where Hokkaido's legendary powder snow meets refined comfort and warm Japanese hospitality.

Carve first tracks from our ski-in, ski-out doorstep, then linger over a 3-hour lunch. Soak in a traditional onsen while the snow does all the falling, and savour Hokkaido's finest flavours, from fresh Otaru seafood to Wagyu from Tokachi. Between the slopes and a lively après-ski, we hope you master the art of enjoying the moment and discover the unique culture and quiet beauty of Japan's northern island.

Know Before You Go

What to pack

What To Pack

Hokkaido welcomes the start of winter in early December, with daytime highs of around 0°C to 5°C in Sapporo and Otaru and night-time lows of about −5°C. Up in the mountains at Kiroro, expect it to be colder, often well below freezing and down to −10°C or lower on the upper slopes.

Health & Safety Notice

As with many winter destinations in Japan, seasonal illnesses such as Acute Gastroenteritis (AGE) can occasionally occur during the colder months. While the risk remains low, we encourage everyone to practise good hand hygiene and make use of the sanitisation facilities available throughout the resort.

Club Med resorts maintain comprehensive hygiene and sanitation protocols and continuously monitor health and safety conditions to provide a safe and enjoyable stay for all guests. Should anyone feel unwell during the trip, our team will be available to assist and support.

To help minimise the risk of illness, we encourage guests to:

  • Wash hands regularly with soap and water.
  • Use hand sanitiser provided throughout the resort.
  • Maintain good personal hygiene, particularly before meals.
  • Rest and seek medical attention if feeling unwell.

Club Med 101

Club Med is truly a unique experience, not only through our pioneering spirit with unparalleled sites and pristine destinations but also our lingo. Here’s a quick cheat sheet to help you:

  • CDV/Chief of Village → the resort’s General Manager
  • G.Os = Great Organizers → Our resort team, here to ensure you have the best stay
  • G.Ms = Great Members → You!
  • Village = Resort
  • Happy to Care = Our sustainability actions and practices
  • Number 45 = You’ll see this on t-shirts and signage in the resort

In 1995, to celebrate Club Med’s 45th anniversary, our resorts sold t-shirts with the number “45” on them. It was a wild success with our clients – and they kept asking for more. Over the years, we have added new designs, asked our clients to design them and began adding the resort names. Thus, creating a legacy and a collector's piece that enables our clients to show which resorts they have visited!
